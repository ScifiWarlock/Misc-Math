\section{External Forces}
When we talk about forces acting on an object and free body diagrams, we assume these are the only forces on the object. This however is not necessarily true. We shall make the following distinction:
\begin{enumerate}
\item External forces are those that act upon the object/system from outside
\item Internal forces are those experienced by members of the object
\end{enumerate}
In equilibrium situations, both the interval and external forces balance; however, in free body diagrams, we only display the external forces (we assume the internal forces are in equilibrium even though the external forces may not).\\

Throughout this series of notes, we shall explicitly distinguish the system from the environment.
